Month 4 narrative (what we are building)

  1. Core thesis unchanged: masked diffusion (LLaDA) for query expansion; semantic anchoring.
  2. Agentic angle (in progress): treat expansion methods as tools; let a router decide when to call them.
  3. Why now: QUID is domain-dependent - always expanding is the wrong default on some query types.
  4. What we will do next: larger BEIR slices, stronger critique/retry, learned router toward oracle, preprint polish.
