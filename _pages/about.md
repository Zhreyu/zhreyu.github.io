I have experience in research, production environments, and have successfully scaled my projects to reach a significant number of users.

beautiful.

Outside the academic world, you'll probably find me listening to music, making some beats of my own, exploring something fascinating yet completely random, or immersed in my [playstation](https://youtu.be/FBhzFgfI_Tw).
